Data & methodology

This dataset is research-driven and open. Every record lists its sources; missing values are explicit null and are never invented. Estimated values are flagged with *_estimated: true.

Confidence tiers

  • Confirmed — operational and officially documented (operator page, regulatory filing, OSM/Wikidata with coordinates).
  • Probable — strong evidence the site exists or is under construction, but not officially confirmed at that exact location (news, satellite imagery, permits).
  • Theorized — announced, rumoured, planned or speculative (e.g. proposed AI mega-campuses).

Sources

  • OpenStreetMap (Overpass API): telecom=data_center, building=data_center
  • Wikidata SPARQL: items that are an instance of data center (Q671224) with coordinates
  • Curated research: hyperscaler region pages (AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, Oracle, IBM, Alibaba), colocation providers (Equinix, Digital Realty, NTT, Switch, QTS, Vantage, CyrusOne, Iron Mountain, STACK, Colt, Global Switch, DATA4, Aruba…), Meta/Apple/xAI/OpenAI campuses, government/HPC sites and crypto-mining facilities.

Limitations

  • Cloud region markers are placed at the region's primary metro area; a region spans multiple availability zones whose exact locations are not public.
  • Power capacity is only shown when publicly reported; most sites have null. Headline MW totals are therefore a lower bound.
  • OSM coverage is community-driven and uneven across countries.
